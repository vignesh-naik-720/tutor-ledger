# CODEBENDERS
**ACE BUILDERS' HACKATHON 2026**

## Problem Statement 1 | Small Business Operations
### The One-Person Back Office

#### The Problem
Micro-businesses such as freelancers, shopkeepers, tutors and small clinics run on WhatsApp chats, notebooks and spreadsheets. Invoices go unsent, payments go unchased, and the owner cannot say who owes what. Worse, they cannot tell whether there will be enough cash next month to cover rent, salaries and supplier bills. Small businesses rarely fail from a lack of customers. They fail because money arrives late and bills arrive on time.

#### Who It Is For
A solo or 1-5 person business with no accounting or admin staff. Pick one specific type (for example a home tutor, a salon or a freelance designer) and build for them.

#### Your Challenge
Build a product that gives this owner a working back office and a clear view of their money: manage customers, invoices and payment follow-ups in one place, and see, predict and act on the cash position over the next 30-90 days.

#### Your Solution Must Include
* A working flow from creating an invoice to tracking its payment, with assisted follow-ups such as drafted reminders
* A dashboard that answers "who owes me, how much, and since when?"
* A 30-90 day cash forecast built from invoices and upcoming bills (sample data is fine)
* At least one "what if" scenario, such as a key customer paying 15 days late
* Alerts that lead to a clear next action, such as "chase this invoice today"

#### Questions to Get You Thinking
* What does the owner do on a bad day, when five customers are late at once?
* What is the minimum data an owner will actually enter, and how can it get in with the least effort?
* Who pays for this, how much per month, and why would they switch from a notebook?
